What Happens Next

  1. Review the budget, share feedback

    Tell us what feels right, what feels high, and any suggestions. If there are questions, we can jump on a call any time to talk them through. Once we've confirmed the shortlist of items together — that's when your final selection takes shape — we'll move to the next step.

  2. Prepare your detailed design plan

    We'll put together a room-by-room plan with full specifications, accurate measurements, factory customization options, and a to-scale 2D floor-plan rendering (from plans you provide). For pieces with showroom samples, we'll share high-definition photos and videos.

  3. You decide when it's ready — and when to order

    We'll take the time needed to refine the proposal until it meets your needs. When you're confident in the final solution, you can order remotely, or visit Foshan first to see pieces in person before confirming your order.
