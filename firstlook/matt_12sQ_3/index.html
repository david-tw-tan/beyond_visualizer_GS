Regarding pricing

Budgets differ — including where you want to invest more. These figures reflect premium factories; if anything feels high, tell us and we can adjust pieces or suppliers. A practical approach: protect spend on the main anchors, and keep accessories more flexible.

All figures are ex-factory only — they do not include international shipping, duties, or installation, which are priced separately.

What Happens Next

  1. Review the budget, share feedback

    Tell us what feels right, what feels high, and any suggestions. If there are key questions, we can jump on a short call to talk them through.

  2. Confirm the budget feels right

    When you're comfortable with the direction, let us know with a refundable US$500 deposit — so we can move to the next stage.

  3. We develop your bespoke solution

    We prepare a detailed, room-by-room proposal with specs, factory customization support, and a to-scale 2D floor-plan rendering (from plans you provide). Where samples are ready in the showroom, we'll also share high-definition photos and videos.

  4. You decide whether to place an order

    We'll take the time needed to refine the proposal until it meets your needs — no rush. When you're confident in the final solution, you can place the order remotely, or visit Foshan first to see pieces in person. For any orders placed, your $500 deposit is deducted from the total. Beyond Showrooms charges a 15% service fee on the ex-factory price of any final order; shipping and duties are arranged separately.
